## Notes

- Three cards under "What I build" are equal weight on desktop (3-up), stack to 1 column on phone — no card should read as more "finished" than another just because of layout order.
- The M365 setup card needs a visibly distinct "not yet available" treatment (not just italic text) so it doesn't read as a live offering — ties to the status-badge pattern used everywhere else on the site, not a one-off style.
- No price shown anywhere on this page, per CLAUDE.md §3 — confirmed still correct as of this wireframe pass.
- "Proof" currently has exactly one case study to link (Little Saporito Press). The payroll system could join this section later if/when it gets its own case study — not now, since it was reframed as an undeployed personal project, not client work.
